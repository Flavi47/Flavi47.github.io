The purpose and limits of this personal application.

AboutPrivacyTerms

Personal-use service

Market Agent Briefings prepares news summaries and explanations for its authorized owner. It is not offered here as a public subscription service. Viewing this website does not create an account, grant Google permissions or authorize a payment.

Advisory information

Briefings distinguish publisher reporting from model-generated interpretation and uncertainty. They are informational, not personalized financial advice, trade instructions or a promise of market performance. Verify consequential claims and prices using the linked source and instrument details.

Source coverage, quote entitlements and market hours vary. Prices may refer to an earlier observation, a prior session or a futures contract. Missing coverage must not be treated as confirmation that no event or market change occurred.

Email authorization

Email delivery requires the owner's separate Google consent. The configured email identity and recipient remain fixed by the service. Consent is limited to sending email and basic account identity; it does not include reading the owner's mailbox. The privacy notice describes data use and revocation.

Availability and corrections

Upstream services, revoked or expired authorization, source restrictions and service failures may delay or prevent a briefing. Delivery acknowledgment is not proof of inbox receipt. Retained reports describe their original observation period; a catch-up message is not a new live market update.

Trading remains separate

The news application does not authorize trades, change trading risk limits or clear operational safeguards. Market forecasts and explanations remain advisory.

Contact and changes

For this personal application, contact the developer through the support contact shown on its Google consent screen. These information pages should be updated when the application's purpose, permissions or data practices change.
