Privacy notice

How the personal news assistant uses Google authorization.

AboutPrivacyTerms

Purpose and audience

This application is operated for its authorized owner's personal news briefings. It does not offer public account registration. The support contact is the developer contact shown on Google's consent screen for Market Agent Briefings.

Google information accessed

  • Basic identity and email-address information, to verify the approved Gmail identity.
  • OAuth authorization tokens, to obtain permission to send requested briefings.
  • Gmail send responses, including delivery identifiers and errors, to track delivery and prevent unintended repeats.

Requested access is limited to sending email and basic identity/email information. The application does not request access to read or search mailbox contents, modify or delete Gmail messages, or access Drive and Contacts.

Use and service providers

Google information is used to identify the approved account, send its requested briefings, maintain delivery records and diagnose failures. Gmail authorization is processed by Google's OAuth and Gmail APIs. Cloud credentials are stored in Google Secret Manager with access restricted to the existing mailer runtime identity and authorized administration.

Local enrollment protects the saved authorization using Windows DPAPI. Authorization credentials are not placed in news-analysis prompts, Telegram messages, public pages or source control. Google API information is not sold, used for advertising or supplied for model training.

News analysis may use Google Cloud Vertex AI and connected news sources. Owner questions may be processed to provide requested explanations. These functions do not require Gmail mailbox-read access or access to Gmail authorization secrets.

Retention and revocation

Delivery records are retained under the application's configured retention rules. Encrypted credentials and historical secret versions may remain until the operator removes them; an authorization deadline does not itself delete stored credentials. Access can be revoked through Google Account connections. The operator can also disable email and remove stored authorization through the application's administration procedures.

Google API policy

Google information is used only for the requested email function and related account verification and delivery controls. Its handling is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This information website

These pages contain no sign-in form, analytics script or advertising tracker. A hosting provider may process standard request metadata when serving the pages. No Gmail permission is granted by viewing this website.
