What this application does

Market Agent Briefings collects eligible market and economic reporting and prepares source-linked briefings and advisory explanations. It covers crypto, equities, commodities, monetary policy and significant geopolitical developments when evidence is available.

Briefings are delivered to the authorized owner through private Telegram messages and email. The application is for personal use; this information site does not provide public registration, a mailbox or a trading service.

Why Google authorization is requested

The email feature requests permission to send email through the approved Gmail account, along with basic account identity and email-address information. That identity is checked against the configured sender and recipient.

The application does not request permission to read, search, modify or delete your Gmail messages. It does not request Google Drive or Contacts access.

News and analysis are advisory. Reporting may be delayed, incomplete or corrected. Dated prices and futures references must not be interpreted as live spot prices. News does not authorize trading or risk changes.

Learn more and manage access

Read the privacy notice for Google data handling and the terms of use for the service's limits. The developer contact is the support contact shown for this application on Google's consent screen.

You can review or revoke Google access in Google Account connections. Revoking access stops email authorization; it does not close your Gmail account.
